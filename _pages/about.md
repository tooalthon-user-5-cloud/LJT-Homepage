I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.ust.hk), supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. in Computer Science.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in:

* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
